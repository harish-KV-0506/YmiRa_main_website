Projects

Smart Bin Level Detection System

2026 · IoT prototype

  • Turns an ordinary dustbin into a real-time IoT node that measures its own fill level.
  • Computes fill level from ultrasonic depth readings on a NodeMCU and opens the lid automatically with a servo.
  • Sends instant email and push alerts through Blynk and Firebase when the bin reaches its threshold.
  • Cuts manual inspection effort and fuel use by improving collection routing logic.

AquaSmart

2026 · Intelligent water purification and zero-waste smart tap

  • An AI and IoT water conservation system that eliminates the dirty water a tap wastes before clean flow starts.
  • A multi-sensor array (TDS, turbidity, flow, temperature) judges purity in real time and drives solenoid valves to send dirty water to reuse or flush tanks.
  • A Blynk and ThingSpeak dashboard tracks water quality live and supports predictive filter maintenance.
